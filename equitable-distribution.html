Dividing property and debt

Property division is not just a list of belongings.

Divorce property issues can involve real estate, retirement accounts, vehicles, bank accounts, debts, business interests, personal property and questions about when and how property was acquired.

Website copy cannot decide how a court would divide property. A clear inventory helps the first discussion focus on what is known, what is missing and what needs attention.

SVMB Law helps Lehigh Valley divorce clients organize property records, identify disputed assets and debts, prepare for settlement discussions and understand how property issues overlap with support, custody and court timing.

Do not guess at values.

Bring statements, appraisals, tax records, payoff records, account documents and ownership papers when available.

Useful first-call details

  • Real estate
  • Retirement accounts
  • Bank and investment accounts
  • Debts and loans

Financial recordkeeping

Ownership, timing and value can all matter.

For each asset or debt, identify whose name is on the account or title, when it was acquired, current value if known, current balance and whether any records show source of funds.

Keep statements complete. A partial screenshot may not show account owner, date, account number, balance history or transactions that matter for review.

Real estate

Bring deeds, mortgages, appraisals, tax bills, closing statements, leases, repair records and payoff information.

Accounts and retirement

Gather bank, investment, retirement, pension and benefit statements with visible names, dates and balances.

Debt

Bring credit-card statements, loan documents, tax debt records, student loan papers and any payoff or collection notices.

Business interests

If a business is involved, gather ownership records, tax returns, profit-and-loss records, bank statements and debts.

Separate property questions

Bring records tied to premarital property, gifts, inheritances, tracing documents or agreements if those issues exist.

Before contacting the office

Have the county, divorce status, service status, court dates, main disputed items and involved party names ready.
