Consultation Checklist

  • County, court, agency and case number if known
  • Next hearing, deadline or response date
  • Names of involved parties and witnesses
  • Copies of notices, orders, letters and messages

Criminal or DUI matters

Bring complaints, citations, bail papers, PennDOT notices, summonses, warrants, hearing notices and any photos, videos or messages.

Family matters

Bring existing orders, income records, parenting schedules, school information, expense records, messages and proposed schedule ideas.

Civil or injury matters

Bring contracts, leases, photos, medical records, bills, insurance letters, claim numbers, estimates and communications.

Employment matters

Bring unemployment notices, hearing dates, termination letters, pay records, disciplinary records, policies and relevant emails or texts.
