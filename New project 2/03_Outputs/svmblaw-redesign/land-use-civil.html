Civil disputes

Facts, documents and deadlines drive the strategy.

Before a dispute reaches court or a local board, written notices, contracts, permits, property records and communications may determine what options are available.

The firm assists with land use, landlord-tenant, contracts, tax assessments, property issues, business disputes and general civil litigation.
