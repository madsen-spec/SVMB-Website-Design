# SVMB Law Website Review and Redesign Notes

Research performed: April 30, 2026.

## Current site observations

- The current home page identifies the firm as a Lehigh Valley law firm handling criminal defense, DUI, child custody, divorce and personal injury matters.
- The live site provides the core contact facts: SVMB Law Offices, 680 Wolf Avenue, Easton, Pennsylvania 18042; info@svmblaw.com; 610-258-3757; fax 610-438-4139.
- The site has useful attorney biographies and client resources, but the page hierarchy is thin. Key visitor paths are split across menus and repeated footer content.
- The "Latest News" area shows "Nothing found," which weakens trust and should be removed or replaced with maintained resources.
- The current contact form is not visible in text-only review and the live page says JavaScript is required for some forms. The redesign keeps phone and email immediately visible and makes the form progressively enhanced.
- Some public PDFs appear to contain older contact details. Before launch, resource PDFs should be checked for current phone, fax, address, disclaimers and revision date.

## Research points applied

- Pennsylvania Rule of Professional Conduct 7.1 prohibits false or misleading communications about a lawyer or legal services. The prototype avoids unsupported guarantees, "best lawyer" claims and unexplained specialist language.
- Pennsylvania Rule of Professional Conduct 7.2 permits communication through media but requires geographic location disclosure and the name/contact information of at least one responsible lawyer or law firm. The prototype repeats the Easton office and responsible law firm in the footer.
- Rule 7.2 also limits specialist implications and requires care around portrayals, paid endorsements, fee statements and referral advertising. The prototype does not include testimonials, dramatized scenes, specific fees or contingent-fee claims.
- Google's SEO Starter Guide emphasizes useful, well-organized content, descriptive titles, clear link text, unique meta descriptions, logical site structure and quality images near relevant text. The prototype uses descriptive headings, practice-area sections and LegalService structured data.
- Google Business Profile guidance identifies relevance, distance and prominence as local ranking factors and recommends complete, accurate business details, current hours, reviews and photos. The prototype makes name, address, phone, office location and services consistent and visible.
- WCAG 2.2 frames accessibility around perceivable, operable, understandable and robust content. The prototype uses semantic landmarks, skip link, visible focus states, labeled form controls, responsive layout and adequate button target sizes.

## Launch checklist

- Attorney review of all practice-area language for Rule 7.1 and Rule 7.2 compliance.
- Confirm each attorney profile is current, including admissions, counties, awards and practice focus.
- Replace or update public resource PDFs that show old contact data.
- Connect the consultation form only to an approved intake workflow.
- Add real office hours if the firm wants them shown in Google Business Profile and schema data.
- Create dedicated pages for high-value local searches, such as criminal defense in Easton, DUI in Northampton County, custody in Lehigh County and PennDOT appeals.
- Add privacy policy, terms/disclaimer page, accessibility statement and cookie/analytics notice if tracking is used.
