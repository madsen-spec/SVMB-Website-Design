DUI and traffic

Protect your license, record and next court deadline.

DUI and traffic matters can involve both court proceedings and separate license consequences. Paperwork from the officer, court and PennDOT should be reviewed carefully.

Save every notice

  • Citations and criminal complaints
  • Chemical-test or refusal paperwork
  • PennDOT suspension letters
  • Hearing notices and appeal deadlines

Driving consequences

A traffic case can affect more than a fine.

License suspensions, points, insurance consequences, employment concerns and criminal penalties may be involved. The response depends on the charge, driving history, county and timing.

The firm handles DUI charges, summary traffic matters, license-related issues and appeals where court or PennDOT deadlines require a prompt response.

Common issues

  • DUI charges and ARD eligibility questions
  • License suspensions and restoration concerns
  • Speeding, reckless driving and careless driving citations
  • Summary appeals and magisterial district court hearings
  • Commercial driving and employment-related concerns

PennDOT deadlines matter.

A PennDOT notice may create a separate appeal deadline even if a criminal case is still pending. Bring all letters from PennDOT to the consultation.

Useful first-call details

  • Date and county of the stop
  • Charges listed on the paperwork
  • License status and prior DUI history
  • Next court or PennDOT deadline

DUI charges

DUI cases may involve stop issues, testing issues, ARD eligibility, mandatory penalties, license consequences and treatment requirements. Early review helps identify the available paths.

Traffic citations

A citation can affect points, license status and employment. Summary appeals and hearings have deadlines, so review should happen before the response date passes.
