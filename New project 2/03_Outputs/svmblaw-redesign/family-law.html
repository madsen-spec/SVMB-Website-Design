Family law

Practical guidance for custody, divorce, support and protection matters.

Family court decisions can affect parenting time, finances, safety, housing and daily routines. Preparation should be focused, factual and organized.

Bring the papers that matter

  • Existing custody, support or divorce orders
  • Conference, hearing or filing notices
  • Income records and expense information
  • Relevant messages, photos and calendars

Family court

Clear goals help manage difficult facts.

Family law matters often involve urgent emotions and long-term consequences. The first step is separating immediate safety or deadline issues from the broader dispute.

The firm assists with custody, divorce, support, alimony, PFA matters, contempt issues and related family court proceedings.

Common family law matters

  • Custody complaints, modifications and relocation concerns
  • Child support, spousal support and alimony
  • Divorce pleadings, property issues and agreements
  • PFA filings, defense and contempt concerns
  • Grandparents' custody rights and related petitions

Keep communications organized.

Texts, emails, school notices, medical records and calendars can become important. Save them in a way that shows date, sender and context.

Before a custody conference

  • Know the current schedule
  • List specific concerns and examples
  • Gather school and childcare details
  • Identify realistic schedule proposals

Custody and parenting time

Custody issues usually require a specific schedule, facts about the child, and a clear explanation of what arrangement serves the child's best interests.

Support and divorce

Support and divorce issues depend on income, expenses, assets, debts and timing. Accurate financial records help avoid avoidable delay.
