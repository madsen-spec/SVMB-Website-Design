How to prepare

Useful information before you call.

Good preparation starts with documents. Court papers, notices, orders, citations, contracts, photographs, messages, medical records and agency letters can change the advice you receive.

When you contact the firm, be ready to identify the county, the names of involved parties, the next known deadline and whether any hearing, arraignment, conference or response date is already scheduled.

For urgent matters

Call the office rather than relying on email or a web form. Deadlines and court appearances should be addressed promptly.
