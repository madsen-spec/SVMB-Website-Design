## Limitations

- This is a static prototype, not a CMS theme or deployed website.
- The contact form is intentionally non-submitting and must be connected to an approved intake workflow before launch.
- Attorney profiles and practice descriptions were based on the current public website and should be attorney-reviewed before publication.
- Resource PDFs were not fully audited. Some public PDFs may contain older contact details and should be reviewed before being linked from a live replacement site.
- No live website, Google Business Profile, email, court, billing or client records were modified.
