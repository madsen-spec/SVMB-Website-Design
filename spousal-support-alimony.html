Divorce and support

The label matters, but the records matter more.

Spousal support, alimony pendente lite and alimony are related but not identical. The right starting point depends on whether a divorce has been filed, whether a support claim is already pending and what the court papers say.

Income, expenses, earning history, health insurance, debt, household needs, custody schedules and property issues can all affect support discussions. A support call is more productive when the actual notice and financial records are available.

SVMB Law helps Lehigh Valley clients organize support papers, prepare for conferences, understand how support overlaps with divorce and decide what records need attention first.

Start with the notice.

The court or Domestic Relations notice may identify the type of proceeding, date, records to bring and whether a support order already exists.

Useful first-call details

  • Support type
  • Conference or hearing date
  • Income records
  • Divorce status

Financial preparation

Support issues often overlap with property, debt and custody.

Support and divorce issues can move together. A support conference may require records that also matter for divorce planning, including income, household expenses, insurance, debts and family-court orders.

If there are related custody or protection orders, bring them too. Those papers can affect residence, parenting time, household expenses and timing.

Support papers

Bring Domestic Relations notices, support complaints, conference orders, hearing notices and any existing support order.

Income records

Gather pay records, tax returns, W-2s, 1099s, benefit information, unemployment records and self-employment records if applicable.

Expenses and debts

Prepare housing, insurance, utilities, childcare, medical, loan, credit-card and recurring household expense information.

Divorce status

Identify whether a divorce complaint has been filed, whether service is complete and whether property or custody issues are also pending.

Existing orders

Bring custody, support, PFA, divorce and other family-court orders that may affect support planning.

Before contacting the office

Have the county, conference date, order status, employment information, filing status and involved party names ready.
