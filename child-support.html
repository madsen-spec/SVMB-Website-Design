Support preparation

Support discussions depend on records, not estimates.

A child-support discussion usually starts with income, the custody schedule, health insurance, childcare costs and any current order or Domestic Relations notice. Estimates can help orient the conversation, but pay records, benefit information, childcare bills and the actual notice usually matter more.

SVMB Law helps Lehigh Valley parents organize support papers, identify the issues for a conference or hearing and gather the records needed to address support, modification or enforcement questions.

Bring actual pay records.

Pay stubs, tax returns, W-2s, benefit records and self-employment records are more useful than verbal estimates.

Useful first-call details

  • Gross and net income records
  • Insurance and childcare costs
  • Custody schedule
  • Existing support orders

Domestic Relations notices

The notice usually tells you what to bring and when to appear.

If Domestic Relations scheduled a conference or hearing, bring the notice and every attachment. It may list records that need to be produced, deadlines and instructions for appearing.

If income recently changed, gather documents showing the date and reason for the change. Job loss, new employment, overtime, commissions, self-employment and benefits can all require different records.

Income records

Bring recent pay stubs, tax returns, W-2s, 1099s, benefit information and self-employment records if applicable.

Custody schedule

Support may be affected by the parenting schedule. Bring the custody order and the schedule actually being followed.

Childcare and insurance

Gather daycare bills, receipts, health insurance premiums, coverage information and recurring child-related costs.

Existing orders

Bring any current child support, spousal support, alimony, custody or divorce orders that may affect the calculation.

Modification questions

If income, custody or expenses changed, organize records showing when and why the change occurred.

Before contacting the office

Have the county, Domestic Relations notice, conference date, employer information, order status and party names ready.
