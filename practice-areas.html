Find the right starting point.

Most people contact a lawyer because something happened, not because they know the legal category. A charge, ticket, custody paper, workplace decision, injury, property dispute, agency notice or court filing can all raise different next steps.

Start with the page closest to what happened. If more than one issue is involved, the office can help sort which paper or date should be read first after a conflict check.

What helps us start

  • What happened or what paper arrived.
  • The county, court, agency or municipality.
  • Any hearing, appeal, filing or response date.

How to prepare

Information to have before you call.

A good first call starts with what arrived, what happened and what date is next. Court papers, notices, orders, citations, contracts, photographs, messages, medical records, insurance letters and agency notices can change the first discussion.

When you contact the firm, be ready to identify the county, names of involved parties and whether any hearing, arraignment, conference, appeal or response date is already scheduled. For Northampton County matters, include whether the paperwork comes from the Court of Common Pleas, a magisterial district court, Domestic Relations, PennDOT, unemployment compensation or another agency.

For urgent dates

Call the office rather than relying only on email. Deadlines and court appearances should be addressed promptly.
