DUI and traffic FAQ

Questions people often ask before calling.

Is a PennDOT letter separate from the criminal case?

Often, yes. A court case and PennDOT license issue may have different steps and deadlines. Bring both court papers and PennDOT notices so the license and criminal timelines can be evaluated together.

What should I bring after a DUI arrest?

Bring the complaint, citations, bail papers, testing paperwork, refusal warnings, license notices, hearing notices and any ARD, treatment or interlock information. If you received documents by mail, keep the envelopes.

Can a traffic ticket affect my license?

Some traffic matters can affect points, suspensions, insurance, employment and CDL status. Evaluate the citation before deciding how to respond, especially if you already have points or prior suspensions.

What if I already received a suspension notice?

Call promptly and identify the date on the notice, the reason listed and any restoration or appeal information included with the letter. A suspension notice should not be ignored because the license issue may continue even while the court matter is pending.
